Done. One last step: turn off the old tracker's nightly rebuild

Your old tracker still rebuilds every night, and if phone sync was on there, it keeps sending last year's families to your Google Contacts. Turn it off there:

  1. Open the old tracker (button below).
  2. Go to 🚀 App Menu → ⚙️ Settings.
  3. Newer trackers: under Live updates, set "Rebuild sheets" to "Only when I click Build", and under Contacts & sync untick "Sync contacts to my phone every night". Older trackers: turn on "Disable Nightly Sync Entirely" and turn off "Sync Contacts to Phones (Nightly)".
  4. Save.

The old tracker was also marked as replaced. If it is a newer version, it turns its nightly rebuild off by itself, but checking Settings makes sure.
