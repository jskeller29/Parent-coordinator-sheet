Some things didn't come over:

    Done. One last step: stop the old tracker's nightly rebuild

    Your old tracker still rebuilds every night, and if phone sync is on there, it keeps sending its families to your Google Contacts.

    If the old tracker is a newer version, it now pauses by itself. When you open it, it asks "Keep this tracker paused?": click Yes.

    An older tracker doesn't ask. Turn it off in its Settings:

    1. Open the old tracker (button below).
    2. Go to 🚀 App Menu → ⚙️ Settings.
    3. Older trackers: turn on "Disable Nightly Sync Entirely" and turn off "Sync Contacts to Phones (Nightly)". Newer trackers: under Live updates, set "Rebuild sheets" to "Only when I click Build", and under Contacts & sync untick "Sync contacts to my phone every night".
    4. Save.
    Open the old tracker
