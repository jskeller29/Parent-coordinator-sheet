Before you start

What the tracker does

The PC Tracker is a Google Sheet with a script attached. You load your student exports once, and the tracker builds the rest: a family directory, a contact log that fills in student details as you type, a history of every contact per student, and your PCAR counts. If you want, it can also put the school directory on your phone through Google Contacts.

Kind of tabTabsWhat you do there
Tabs you type inContact Log, Events, Notes, Send OutLog calls, meetings and events.
Tabs that fill themselvesStudent List, Directory, Combined Contact Tracking, Parents Divided, Phone Contacts, PCARRead them. A build rewrites them, so don't type in them.
Data and settingsRaw Data, BackendRaw Data holds your exports. Backend is hidden and holds the tracker's own settings.

Get your copy

Every coordinator works in their own copy. Click Make your copy in the box at the top of this guide, then Make a copy. (Or open the PC Tracker template folder, open the newest file, its name ends in "(BLANK)" and a date, and choose File → Make a copy.) The copy is private and lives in your own Google Drive.

The same template is for everyone. If you used the tracker last year, you bring your old data over during setup.

Want to see one filled in first? "See an example tracker" (the box at the top of this guide, the 👋 Welcome tab, and 🚀 App Menu → 📖 Open User Guide) opens a tracker with made-up students. Look around in it, but keep your real work in your own copy.

Part 1: Setting up

Quick start

Setup takes about 10 minutes.

  1. Make your copy (see above). It opens on the 👋 Welcome tab, the only tab showing.
  2. Read the Welcome tab. It explains each permission Google will ask for.
  3. Click 🚀 App Menu → 🚨 Initial Setup (Run Once) and allow it.
  4. Follow the Getting Started checklist that opens on the right.

Right after Initial Setup, a window asks whether this is your first PC Tracker. If you used one last year, choose "No, bring over last year's tracker" (Part 3).

Step 1: Open your copy

Until setup is done, your copy shows only the 👋 Welcome tab. It explains the permissions, lists the setup steps and links to this guide and the bug report form. A message at the bottom of the screen points you to Initial Setup. After setup, the Welcome tab stays and shows what to do next. It comes back each time you open the sheet, so a window you closed by accident is never a dead end. It goes away by itself once your sheets are built.

Step 2: What Google will ask for

Initial Setup asks Google for permission once. Google shows its own screens before the tracker can show anything, so here is what each permission is for, in Google's words:

Google's wordingWhat the tracker uses it for
See, edit, create and delete all your Google Sheets spreadsheetsBuilding and updating the tabs in this tracker. It also reads the shared Version Tracker sheet to tell you about a new version, and opens last year's tracker when you use Import Old Data.
See, edit, download and permanently delete your contactsOnly the phone sync, which is off until you turn it on in Settings. It only adds, changes and removes contacts in its own "School Directory" label.
See, edit, create and delete all of your Google Drive filesSaving your end-of-year PDF archive next to the tracker, finding the newest User Guide and template links, and finding your old tracker when you bring it over.
Connect to an external serviceOnly the end-of-year PDF, which it downloads from Google's own export page.
Allow this application to run when you are not presentThe updates when you type in the Contact Log, the nightly rebuild at 10 PM and the update check when you open the sheet.
Display and run third-party web content in prompts and sidebarsThe tracker's own pop-ups and side panels, such as Settings and Input Data Mode. "Third-party" means not made by Google.

All of this happens inside your own Google account. There is no outside server, and nothing about your families is sent to the person who made the tracker. Part 4 has more.

Step 3: Run Initial Setup

  1. Wait for 🚀 App Menu to appear at the top of the screen, after Help. It can take a few seconds. If it isn't there after a minute, reload the page.
  2. Click 🚀 App Menu → 🚨 Initial Setup (Run Once).
  3. "Authorization required": click OK, then choose your Google account.
  4. "Google hasn't verified this app": click Advanced, then "Go to … (unsafe)". Google shows this for tools made by a person rather than a company. It does not mean something is wrong.
  5. On the list of permissions, tick Select all, then click Continue. Setup needs all of them. If one is left unticked, Google asks again.
  6. If nothing happens after that, click 🚨 Initial Setup (Run Once) again.

A "Setting up your tracker" window opens right away. Keep it open: setup takes about 20 seconds. Setup then:

  • turns on the automatic parts (Contact Log updates, the 10 PM rebuild, the update check),
  • keeps you on the Welcome tab, which now shows what to do next. Your everyday tabs (Contact Log, Combined Contact Tracking, PCAR and Directory) stay hidden until your sheets are built, because they are empty until then. You can turn on others later in Settings → Tabs & columns,
  • asks "Is this your first PC Tracker?": "Yes, start fresh" shows the first steps, and "No, bring over last year's tracker" opens the Migration Wizard (Part 3),
  • opens the Getting Started checklist.
Note

Before setup, the sheet won't let you type. You'll see "Action Blocked: Initialization process needs to occur first." Run Initial Setup and it goes away.

Step 4: The Getting Started checklist

The checklist opens on the right after setup and ticks each step off by itself:

  1. Load your student data (Step 5).
  2. Build your sheets. Input Data Mode does this for you.
  3. Check your settings (Part 2).
  4. Log your first parent contact (Part 2).

It opens each time you open the sheet until all four are done, or until you click Finish setup at the bottom of it. You can always reopen it from 🚀 App Menu → ✅ Getting Started. If you close Input Data Mode half way, it opens again by itself the next time you open the sheet, or from 🚀 App Menu → 📥 Input Data Mode. Below the steps, "This tab" explains the tab you are on, and after your first build "Hidden tabs" lists the tabs you can turn on.

Step 5: Load your data

Open 🚀 App Menu → 📥 Input Data Mode, or click Open Input Data Mode in the checklist. It takes you through each export in turn, then two optional steps: Site / Class (the teacher and site for each class) and Your Lists. Your other tabs are hidden while you work. If you close its panel by accident, the yellow tab still says how to bring it back: 🚀 App Menu → 📥 Input Data Mode. It picks up where you left off.

For each export, either click Upload file and pick the file you downloaded, or open the file, select everything (Ctrl+A, Mac ⌘+A), copy it, and paste it anywhere on the temporary tab with Ctrl+Shift+V (Mac ⌘+Shift+V). The data moves into Raw Data on its own. When it is in, the top of the panel says so on the next step, for example "Student Data (ATS) completed!". If you pasted the wrong export, or Excel shortened the numbers (they show as 7.19E+09), the tracker tells you and changes nothing. Skip keeps the old data for that export.

Before the Your Lists step, Input Data Mode builds your sheets. Finish or exit ends it at any step. Save, exit and build my sheets saves what is on the tab, brings your tabs back and builds.

How to download your data

Student Data (required), from ATS / Insight:

  1. Sign in to TeachHub and open Insight.
  2. Go to Tools → Downloader.
  3. Under "Data Available for Download", select only Guardian Contact Information, then download.
  4. In your Downloads, look for the file whose name starts with general_.

Parent Square (optional):

  1. Sign in to ParentSquare and open Admin.
  2. Under Data Assistant, select Parents.
  3. Click Export CSV.
  4. In your Downloads, look for the file named parents.csv.

NYCSA (optional):

  1. Sign in to TeachHub and open Family Access Management.
  2. Export the "Students with Non-NYCSA Account" report.
  3. In your Downloads, look for the file whose name starts with Students_With_NonNycsa_Accts_Rpt_.

Pasting into Raw Data yourself

You can also paste an export straight into the Raw Data tab (turn it on in Settings → Tabs & columns): Student Data into cell A5, Parent Square into AG6, NYCSA into AP2.

Important

Paste values only, with Ctrl+Shift+V or Edit → Paste special → Values only. A normal paste can bring in formatting or formulas that break the build. Input Data Mode does this for you.

Your own lists

On the Your Lists step you can add any other list, such as bus routes, after-school sign-ups, PTA members or trip forms. Click Add another list, give it a name and pick an icon for its tab (📥 unless you pick another one). Then paste the whole sheet (headers too) into the new tab, or upload the file. To change the icon later, click it on the list's card. The side panel then has four steps:

  1. Columns. It finds the header row and guesses what each column is. Fix any guess, and say whether each row is a student or a guardian.
  2. Variables. Pick the other columns to keep (a route, a fee, a form status), name them, and choose where they show: on the phone contact under Additional Information, and as a column in the Student List or Parents Divided.
  3. Match. It works out who each row is: by OSIS first, then name, then phone or email, then best guesses for nicknames and short names. You confirm the rows it isn't sure of. Check a few best guesses before you accept them all.
  4. View. Choose how the values read (one per line or all on one line) and click Save.

Your choices are remembered. When a new version of the list comes in, click Paste new version and only the new rows need you. A list with an OSIS column matches best.

Warning

Anything shown under Additional Information goes into your Google Contacts, and onto your phone after the next sync. Keep private lists (IEP, housing, health) off the phone contacts.

Fixing a student's details

ATS can lag behind real enrollment. To add a student who isn't in ATS yet, or to fix a name or a parent's phone, use 👤 Student Overrides → ✨ New / Edit Student. Your change shows in the Student List, Combined Contact Tracking, Parents Divided and Phone Contacts at the next build. The Directory always shows ATS data only. An override removes itself once ATS has the same information.

Step 6: Build the sheets

Input Data Mode builds your sheets for you. After new data, overrides or lists, build again with 🚀 App Menu → 🏗️ Build Sheets Only. A side panel shows each step as it runs, then a short report: new students, discharged students, and families who signed up for NYCSA or ParentSquare. Click a number to see the names.

After the first build, the full menus appear: ⚙️ Settings, 👤 Student Overrides and 📆 Events. If you don't see them, reload the page.

Note

Raw Data is the only tab you paste exports into. Everything the tracker builds is rebuilt from it, so building again is always safe.

The Contact Log

Log every call, email, meeting and walk-in here, one row each.

A few things happen on their own:

  • When you type a name or OSIS, the rest of the row's student details fill in, for siblings too. If the tracker can't find the name, a message says so. Pasting a whole column of names or OSIS numbers works too.
  • Date, # and Follow-up fill in only where you left them blank. Anything you type stays.
  • A blank row is always kept above the dark END bar. The checkbox on the END bar switches a filter that shows only this week and last week (or only today, see Settings) plus open follow-ups.

Below the END bar is a search box. Type a student's OSIS or name to see their communications before you call.

If you get "Could not find … in the Student List", that student isn't in the sheets yet. Run 🏗️ Build Sheets Only and try again.

Events

Use the Events tab for workshops, conferences and other group events, so they don't crowd the Contact Log. Click 📆 Events → 📋 New Event, fill in the name, type, location and date, and the tracker adds an attendance table. Enter each family by OSIS, student name or guardian name; you can paste a whole column, or several OSIS numbers in one cell separated by commas. Attendance shows in each student's Combined Contact Tracking and in the PCAR.

Build and Full Sync

What Build Sheets does

🏗️ Build Sheets Only works inside the spreadsheet: it rebuilds the Student List, Directory, Combined Contact Tracking and the other tabs from your data.

What Full Sync does

🧱 Full Sync does a build and then updates your Google Contacts. It keeps a "School Directory" label in your contacts matching the Phone Contacts tab: it adds new parents, updates changed ones and removes those who left. A side panel shows the progress. You can close it; the sync keeps going. Large schools may take more than one run, and it picks up where it stopped. With the phone sync on, the nightly run does the same.

Part 3: New versions and the end of the year

Bring over last year's data

Use this at the start of a new school year, and also to move to a new version in the middle of the year. Choose "No, bring over last year's tracker" in the window after Initial Setup, or open 🚀 App Menu → 🔄 Import Old Data before your first build. The wizard lists the trackers it finds in your Google Drive: click yours, or paste its link if it isn't listed, then click Bring it over. It goes through six steps:

  1. Connect to the old sheet. A newer tracker can't be brought into an older one.
  2. Bring over your settings, as they were saved in the old tracker.
  3. Copy the ATS Raw Data.
  4. Copy the Notes tab, events, Send Out, New / Edit Student changes, the Type list and your lists.
  5. Match up the Contact Log, column by column (renamed columns like "Notes" find their new place).
  6. Bring over the Student List history (students who left keep their notes) and rebuild the other tabs.

If a step fails, click Try Again: it goes on from that step. Anything that couldn't come over is listed when the wizard is done.

Your old sheet's tabs are only read, never changed. Keep it until you have checked the new one.

Important

Your old tracker still rebuilds every night, and with phone sync on it keeps sending its families to your Google Contacts. When the wizard is done, a tracker of this version or newer pauses its nightly rebuild and phone sync by itself. The next time you open it, it asks "Keep this tracker paused?": click Yes. (Click No if you still use it.) An older tracker doesn't do this. The wizard shows a button to open it. There, open 🚀 App Menu → ⚙️ Settings, turn on "Disable Nightly Sync Entirely", turn off phone sync and save.

Updates

When you open the sheet, the tracker checks at most every few hours whether a newer version is out, and shows what changed. You can also check with 🚀 App Menu → 🔔 Check for Updates. In that window you can dismiss the update for now, dismiss it until a newer one comes out, ask to hear about major updates only, or stop the automatic check.

An update doesn't change your current sheet. To move to a new version:

  1. Click Get the latest version and make the copy.
  2. In the new copy, run 🚀 App Menu → 🚨 Initial Setup.
  3. Choose "No, bring over last year's tracker", pick your current tracker and click Bring it over.
  4. Your data, notes, lists and settings come with it. Your old tracker then pauses its nightly rebuild (see the box above).

End of Year Rollover

When the school year is over, run 🚀 App Menu → 🔄 End of Year Rollover. It:

  • saves a PDF archive of your Contact Log, Combined Contact Tracking and your lists in the same Drive folder as the tracker,
  • turns off the nightly sync so the old sheet can't change next year's contacts,
  • gives you the link to next year's tracker. Make a copy, run Initial Setup, and choose "No, bring over last year's tracker".

Keep the old sheet and the PDF as your record for the year.

Part 4: Privacy

The person who made the tracker can't see your sheet, your students, your contacts or your Drive. The tracker has no server or database of its own. The script runs inside your Google account, as you.

  • When you allow the script, you give the permission to the script in your own account. Nobody else receives anything.
  • The tracker reads and writes your own spreadsheet, and your own Google Contacts only if you turn on the phone sync.
  • You can read the code: Extensions → Apps Script in the sheet.
  • You can remove access at any time: myaccount.google.com → Security → Your connections to third-party apps & services.

The "Google hasn't verified this app" screen appears for tools like this one that are not published in Google's Marketplace. Part 1, Step 2 lists each permission.

These are the only times the tracker looks outside your sheet:

  • The update check reads a shared, view-only Version Tracker sheet. It sends nothing about your students.
  • Import Old Data lists the trackers in your Drive by name, and opens only the one you pick.
  • A bug report sends only what you type into the form.

Who else can see your copy is up to you. It is in your Drive, and you decide whether to share it.

Part 5: Help

A typical day

  1. Before a call, look the student up in the search box below the Contact Log's END bar.
  2. After a contact, type the name or OSIS in a new row and fill in the notes and the follow-up.
  3. To fix a name or phone, or add a missing student, use 👤 Student Overrides → ✨ New / Edit Student.
  4. When you download a new export, load it with 📥 Input Data Mode.
  5. For a big batch of entries, set Settings → Live updates → Rebuild sheets to "Only when I click Build", then build once at the end.

Tips

  • Siblings are linked by OSIS. To log one contact for several siblings, pick the entry with all their names (for example "Louise & Gene Belcher").
  • Type your own date, # or follow-up and the auto-fill leaves it alone.
  • Hide discharged students only hides them. Purge deletes them for good.
  • If a menu looks out of date, reload the page.
  • The data reminder appears once a day when an export is older than your setting. Change it in Settings → Student data.

Things to avoid

  • Typing in the tabs the tracker builds (Student List, Directory and so on). The next build overwrites it.
  • Renaming or deleting the tracker's tabs, including the hidden Backend tab.
  • Purging discharged students unless you are sure.
  • Deleting your old tracker before you have checked the new one.
