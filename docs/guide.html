What this tool is

The Parent Coordinator (PC) Tracker is a Google Sheets add-on built with Google Apps Script. You paste your student exports into one tab, and the tool automatically builds and maintains everything else: a clean class directory, a per-student contact log, PCAR reporting numbers, and — if you want it — a copy of the directory pushed into your phone through Google Contacts.

It's organized into three kinds of sheets:

  • The Engine — Raw Data and the Backend tab's Type List, where you paste your system exports and customize your dropdowns.
  • The Workspaces — Contact Log and Events, where you'll spend most of your day logging calls and workshops.
  • The Output — automated sheets like Directory, Student List, Combined Contact Tracking, Parents Divided, PCAR, and Phone Contacts. You never type into these — they build themselves.
New here? Do the one-time setup in Part 1, load your data, run Build, and you're running in a few minutes.

Part 1 — Setting Up Your Sheet

Quick Start (4 steps, about 10 minutes)

  1. Make your own copy of the template (it opens a "Make a copy" prompt, or use File → Make a copy). Your copy opens on the 👋 Welcome tab, the only tab showing.
  2. Read what Google will ask. The Welcome tab (and Step 2 below) explains each permission before you click anything.
  3. Click 🚀 App Menu → 🚨 Initial Setup (Run Once) and allow it: OK → your account → Advanced → Go to … (unsafe) → tick Select all → Continue.
  4. Follow your ✅ Getting Started checklist (it opens on the right): Load your student data → Build your sheets → Check your settings → Log your first parent contact.
Moving from last year's tracker? Run Initial Setup first, then use 🚀 App Menu → 🔄 Import Old Data (an upgrade copy opens it by itself). See Part 3.

Step 1 — Make a copy and open it

You always start from a shared template. Making a copy drops a fresh, private spreadsheet into your own Google Drive. The template is never touched by your work, and no one else can see your copy.

Your copy opens on the 👋 Welcome tab. Until setup is done it is the only tab showing: it explains each permission Google will ask for, lists the setup steps, and links to this guide and the bug report form. A message at the bottom of the screen also points you to 🚀 App Menu → 🚨 Initial Setup (Run Once). Initial Setup removes the Welcome tab and brings your tabs back.

Step 3 — Run Initial Setup and allow it

  1. Wait until 🚀 App Menu appears at the top of the screen, next to Help. It takes a few seconds; not there after a minute? Reload the page.
  2. Click 🚀 App Menu → 🚨 Initial Setup (Run Once).
  3. Authorization required: click OK, then choose your Google account.
  4. Google hasn't verified this app: click Advanced, then Go to … (unsafe). "Unverified" means Google hasn't done a formal brand review of a tool made by a person — not that anything is wrong.
  5. On the list of permissions, tick Select all, then click Continue. Setup needs all of them; if one is left unticked, Google asks again for the missing ones.
  6. Nothing happened after that? Click 🚀 App Menu → 🚨 Initial Setup (Run Once) again.

What Initial Setup does:

  • Installs the automatic triggers: instant Contact Log updates, the nightly rebuild/sync (around 10 PM) and the update check when you open the sheet.
  • Records this copy as your original (so a later copy of it starts clean).
  • Removes the Welcome tab and shows your everyday tabs: Contact Log, Combined Contact Tracking, PCAR and Directory (turn on more in ⚙️ Settings → Tabs & columns).
  • Opens the welcome pop-up and your ✅ Getting Started checklist.
🔒 Until setup is done, the sheet is intentionally locked. If you type before running Initial Setup, you'll see "🔒 Action Blocked — Initialization process needs to occur first." That's expected.

Step 4 — Follow the Getting Started checklist

The checklist opens on the right after setup and ticks itself off as you go:

  1. Load your student data — opens 📥 Input Data Mode (Step 5).
  2. Build your sheets — Input Data Mode does this for you (Step 6).
  3. Check your settings — opens ⚙️ Settings (Part 2).
  4. Log your first parent contact — takes you to the Contact Log (Part 2).

It opens by itself each time you open the sheet until all four are done (untick Open this by itself to stop that), and you can always reopen it from 🚀 App Menu → ✅ Getting Started. Under the steps, This tab says what the tab you're on is for, and Hidden tabs lists the tabs you can turn on in ⚙️ Settings → Tabs & columns.

Step 5 — Load your data (📥 Input Data Mode)

Before logging calls, the system needs to know who your students are. Open 🚀 App Menu → 📥 Input Data Mode (or Open Input Data Mode in the checklist) and the sheet walks you through the three exports one at a time, then two optional steps: Site / Class (each class's teacher and site) and Your Lists (below). Your other tabs are hidden while you work. For each export, either click 📂 Upload file and pick the file you downloaded, or open the file, press Ctrl+A (Mac: ⌘+A), copy (Ctrl+C / ⌘+C), and paste anywhere on the temporary tab with Ctrl+Shift+V (Mac: ⌘+Shift+V). The data moves into Raw Data by itself, and the sidebar shows when each one was last updated. It warns you, without changing Raw Data, if you pasted the wrong export or if Excel cut numbers off (like 7.19E+09). Use Skip to keep the old data for an export. Before the Your Lists step, it builds your sheets for you. Finish or exit ends it at any step: Save, exit and build my sheets saves what's on the tab, brings your tabs back and builds. The end screen offers Build sheets now only if something changed since that build, and Next steps back to your Getting Started checklist.

Student Data (ATS / Insight) — required

  1. Go to TeachHub and sign in.
  2. In the search bar, look up and open Insight.
  3. Go to Tools → Downloader.
  4. Under "Data Available for Download," select only Guardian Contact Information, then download. Upload or paste it in Input Data Mode.

Parent Square — optional

  1. Go to ParentSquare, sign in, and open Admin.
  2. Under Data Assistant, select Parents.
  3. Click Export CSV. Upload or paste it in Input Data Mode.

NYCSA — optional

  1. Go to TeachHub and sign in.
  2. Open Family Access Management.
  3. Export the "Students with Non-NYCSA Account" report. Upload or paste it in Input Data Mode.

Pasting straight into Raw Data instead

You can also paste each export into its colored box on the Raw Data tab (show it in ⚙️ Settings → Tabs & columns): Student Data into cell A5, Parent Square into AG6, NYCSA into AP2.

🚨 Pasting into Raw Data? Always paste values only. Use Edit → Paste Special → Values Only (Ctrl+Shift+V). Pasting normally can drag in formatting or formulas that break the build. (Input Data Mode does this for you.)

Your own lists (Input Data Mode, Your Lists step)

Have a list that isn't an ATS export, like bus routes, after-school sign-ups, PTA members or trip forms? On the Your Lists step, click ➕ Add another list, name it, and paste the whole sheet (headers too) into the yellow box on the new 📥 (name) tab, or upload the file (CSV or Excel). The sidebar then takes you through four short steps:

  1. Columns. It finds the header row by itself (title rows above it are fine) and guesses what each column is, shown like OSIS: Column C “OSIS”. Fix a guess with its dropdown, and say whether each row is a student or a guardian. A name can be one column (“LAST, FIRST” is fine) or two (first + last).
  2. Variables. The other columns (a route, a fee, a form status). Tick keep, give it a name, and choose what a blank cell shows and what everyone who isn't on the list gets: nothing, or your own text (like “No bus”). Then choose where it shows: under Additional Information on the phone contact (above Communications), and/or as a column in the Student List (student lists) or Parents Divided (guardian lists).
  3. Match. It works out who each row is: OSIS first, then names, then phone or email, then best guesses for short names and nicknames (Sam → Samuel). Rows it isn't sure of are listed for you: confirm a best guess, pick one of several possible students, search the whole Student List, or skip the row. ✓ Accept all best guesses takes them all at once; check a couple first, because best guesses can be wrong.
  4. View. How the kept values read on the phone contact and in the Student List / Parents Divided: one per line or all on one line, names in bold or not, with a live preview. Click Save here.

Your picks are remembered: when you get a new version of the list, click Paste new version and only the new rows need you. The list's tab shows who each row matched (✔ Matched / How). What you keep reaches the Student List, Parents Divided and the phone contacts at the next build; Combined Contact Tracking doesn't change. Add as many lists as you like.

⚠️ Anything shown under Additional Information goes into your Google Contacts. It is on the guardian's contact (and your phone) after the next sync. Keep private lists (IEP, housing, health) off, or keep them on their own tab only.

Missing or wrong data? Use the New/Edit Student override

District databases like ATS can lag behind real enrollment. If you need to add a student or fix contact info before it hits ATS, use 👤 Student Overrides → New / Edit Student. When you build, your overrides are applied to the Student List, Combined Contact Tracking, Parents Divided, Phone Contacts, and the Ghost Typist. The Directory tab and the build report's new / discharged students always show ATS (Raw Data) only. Overrides also tidy themselves up: a "New Student" override is removed once ATS has that student, an edit that ATS now matches exactly is removed too, and a student's class always follows Raw Data once they're in ATS. (More in Part 2.)

Customizing dropdowns — the Type List

The optional Type column in the Contact Log is driven by the Type List in the hidden Backend tab (column BL). Unhide Backend and edit that column to set your own conversation-topic categories (for example: Attendance, Behavior, Enrollment, IEP).

Step 6 — Build the sheets

Input Data Mode builds your sheets for you. To build again later (after new data, overrides or lists), run 🚀 App Menu → 🏗️ Build Sheets Only. A progress sidebar generates every derived tab and checks off each step as it goes (reading your data, Student List, Event Log, Combined Contact Tracking, Parents Divided, Directory, Phone Contacts, finishing up), with a line under each saying what it builds. When it finishes, the sidebar shows a short report — new students, discharged students, and families newly signed up for NYCSA or ParentSquare (click a count to see the names). The first successful build adds the full menus right away: ⚙️ Settings, 👤 Student Overrides and 📆 Events (don't see them? reload the page with F5).

The golden rule of this sheet Raw Data is the only tab you paste into. Everything else is generated — so re-running Build always safely rebuilds it. Never hand-type into Student List, Directory, or other generated tabs.

Three things the Contact Log does for you automatically:

  • Ghost Typist. Type — or paste a whole column of — OSIS numbers, student names, or guardian names, and the other identity columns (plus Site and Class, including for siblings) fill in for every row. If it can't match, it warns "Could not find … in the Student List" (after a paste, it lists the ones it couldn't find).
  • Smart auto-fill. Entering real content stamps today's Date, sets # to 1, and Follow-up to "No Followup Needed" — but only where you left them blank. Anything you typed yourself is never overwritten.
  • Auto-adding rows + the master hide filter. A fresh blank row is always kept above the dark "END" bar. The checkbox on the END-bar row turns on a filter that shows only this and last week's rows (or only today's — see Settings), plus anything still marked for follow-up.
If the Ghost Typist says "Could not find … in the Student List" That family isn't compiled yet. Run 🏗️ Build Sheets Only once, then try again.

Build vs. Full Sync — know the difference

CommandWhat it is
🏗️ Build / Rebuild DataAn internal action. Takes your Raw Data and constructs all the automated sheets (Directory, Student List, Parents Divided, Phone Contacts, PCAR). It only organizes data inside the spreadsheet.
🧱 Full Sync with Google ContactsAn external action. Pushes the formatted Phone Contacts tab into your actual Google Contacts via a "School Directory" label — one number per card — so a parent's name, relationship, and student show on caller ID.

The phone sync runs in "mirror" mode — creating, editing, and removing contacts so the label always matches your directory. It runs nightly or on demand via 🧱 Full Sync, with a progress sidebar you can pause, stop, or restart; it's resilient and resumes if it hits Google's time limits.

Part 3 — Migration & Updates

Importing data from an older sheet (Migration Wizard)

Open 🚀 App Menu → 🔄 Import Old Data. (On an "upgrade" copy it opens by itself.) Paste the link to your older PC sheet and click Start Safe Migration. The wizard runs six steps:

  1. Connect to the old sheet (confirms the link works and you have access).
  2. Recover your custom settings (inferred from which tabs were visible in the old sheet).
  3. Migrate your ATS Raw Data.
  4. Transfer your Events, Send Out, and Student Overrides.
  5. Smart-map the Contact Log — it matches columns by their headers, so it still works even if the layout changed, and strips blank/"ghost" rows.
  6. Finalize and rebuild all directories.
Migration is one-directional and non-destructive. It reads your old sheet and writes into the new one. Nothing is deleted from the old sheet — keep it as a backup.

Staying current (the update checker)

Each copy remembers its own build date. Quietly, on open (at most once every few hours), the sheet checks a shared Version Tracker to see whether a newer template has shipped. If so, it pops a short changelog with a link to the newest copy. You can also check any time with 🚀 App Menu → 🔔 Check for Updates.

Inside that dialog you can:

  • Dismiss for now — close it; it pops up again on the next check.
  • Dismiss this update — hide this update until something even newer ships.
  • Major updates only — automatic pop-ups only for big releases (a manual check still shows everything).
  • Stop automatic update checks — silence the on-open checks; the manual menu check always still works.

How to actually adopt an update

Updates don't rewrite your current sheet in place. To move up a version: open the new template link, make a copy, run Initial Setup, then use the Migration Wizard to bring your data across. Your settings come along automatically.

End of Year Rollover

When the year ends, run 🚀 App Menu → 🔄 End of Year Rollover. It:

  • Generates a permanent PDF archive of your Contact Log and Combined Contact Tracking, saved next to your sheet in Drive.
  • Freezes the sheet — turns off nightly and phone sync so it won't overwrite next year's data — and renames it "… – Retired on [date]."
  • Hands you the link to next year's fresh, fast template.
Rollover is safe to run once you're done for the year. It archives and freezes the OLD sheet; you then set up a brand-new copy for the new year. Keep the retired sheet and its PDF as your record.

Part 4 — Security & Privacy

The short version: I cannot see your sheet, your students, your contacts, or your Drive. This tool has no server and no database of mine. The code runs entirely inside your own Google account. Nothing about your families is ever sent to me or anyone else. Once you make a copy, it's yours.

Why that's true

  • It runs as you, on Google's servers. When you click Authorize, you grant permission to the script — running inside your account — not to me. I receive nothing.
  • There's no outside destination for your data. The tool reads and writes your own spreadsheet and (optionally) your own Google Contacts. It doesn't phone home.
  • You can read every line of the code. Open Extensions → Apps Script in the sheet.
  • You can revoke access anytime. Go to myaccount.google.com → Security → Your connections to third-party apps & services, and remove the tool.

Why the "unverified app" warning appears

That screen shows for personal Apps Script tools that aren't published to Google's Marketplace. It means Google hasn't run a formal brand review — not that the tool is unsafe. For a permission-by-permission breakdown, see "What each authorization actually does" in Part 1.

The only things that ever leave your sheet

  • The update check reads a shared, view-only "Version Tracker" spreadsheet to compare dates. It sends nothing about your students.
  • Migration opens an older sheet only when you paste its link, and only because you already have access to it.
  • Bug reports go through a Google Form you choose to fill out — only the words you type are shared.
Who can see your copy? Entirely up to you. Once you make a copy it lives in your Drive; you decide who (if anyone) gets shared access.

Tips, Workflow & Troubleshooting

A good daily workflow

  1. Before a call, use the Contact Log search bar to pull up the family's history.
  2. Made a contact? In a new row, type the OSIS or a name and let the Ghost Typist fill the rest. Add notes and a follow-up flag.
  3. Need to fix a name/phone or add a missing student? Use 👤 Student Overrides → New / Edit Student.
  4. Fresh ATS export? Paste it into Raw Data (Paste Special → Values Only), then run 🏗️ Build Sheets Only.
  5. Big batch? Turn on "Rebuilds: Manual / Nightly Sync Only," then run Build once at the end.

Handy tips

  • Siblings are automatic. The tool joins siblings by OSIS and fills Site/Class for each.
  • Back-dating works. Type your own date, count, or follow-up status and the auto-fill leaves it alone.
  • Hide vs. Purge discharged. "Hide Discharged" is a reversible view filter; "Purge" permanently deletes.
  • Refresh if a menu looks stale. Pressing F5 always reloads the latest menu.
  • Fresh-data reminder. If your ATS, ParentSquare or NYCSA data in Raw Data is older than your reminder setting (a week by default), the sheet shows a reminder once a day when you open it. Change it or turn it off in ⚙️ Settings → Student data.
  • Lists match best with OSIS. If a list you add has an OSIS column, every row with an OSIS matches exactly; names alone need more checking.

Troubleshooting / FAQ

SymptomFix
The custom menus didn't appearWait a few seconds, then press F5. If it's a brand-new copy, run 🚨 Initial Setup first.
I only see the Welcome tabSetup hasn't been run on this copy yet. Click 🚀 App Menu → 🚨 Initial Setup (Run Once); it removes the Welcome tab and shows your tabs.
Google keeps asking for permissionSetup needs every permission on the list. Tick Select all, then Continue (what each one is for: Part 1, Step 2).
"🔒 Action Blocked" when I typeSetup hasn't been run yet. Click 🚀 App Menu → 🚨 Initial Setup (Run Once).
Pasted data looks broken / directory is wrongRe-paste using Edit → Paste Special → Values Only, then run 🏗️ Build Sheets Only.
Ghost Typist: "Could not find … in the Student List"That family isn't compiled yet. Run 🏗️ Build Sheets Only, then try again.
"📡 Background Sync in Progress" warningA rebuild/sync is running. Wait for it to finish before entering new data.
Update checker says I'm up to date but there's a new versionUse 🔔 Check for Updates (it bypasses the throttle). If still wrong, report a bug.
Phone sync created duplicates / didn't finishOpen Full Sync, use Stop then Restart. The sync mirrors the sheet and cleans up on the next full run.
A row on my list says “not found”That student (or guardian) isn't on the Student List. A new student? Add them with 👤 New / Edit Student, build, then open the list again (📥 Input Data Mode → Your Lists → Edit setup). Or pick them by hand with 🔍 Pick from the Student List.
Something's broken or confusingUse 🚀 App Menu → 🐞 Report a Bug, or email jacob.keller@p94m.org.

Do & Don't

✅ Do🚫 Don't
Paste raw data only into Raw Data — as Values Only.Type into Student List, Directory, or other generated tabs — they're overwritten on build.
Use Overrides to fix names, phones, or add students.Rename or delete the generated / hidden tabs (including Backend).
Run Build after big changes.Hand-edit the hidden Backend tab's saved settings.
Keep your old / retired sheet as a backup until the new one is verified.Purge discharged students unless you're certain.

Glossary

TermMeaning
OSISThe unique student ID number from ATS. Used to match students, siblings, and contacts.
ATS / InsightThe NYC DOE student data systems your Raw Data export comes from (via TeachHub).
PCARParent Coordinator Activity Report — the reporting numbers this tool compiles.
Ghost TypistThe auto-fill that completes a Contact Log, Notes, or Events row from a single OSIS or name.
OverrideA correction or addition to student/guardian info without changing raw ATS data.
Stealth ModeAn override flag that keeps a student out of the Override Report.
NYCSA / ParentSquareParent account/communication systems whose exports show each family's sign-up status in Parents Divided and Phone Contacts.
BuildRegenerating all derived tabs from your current data (🏗️ Build Sheets Only).
Your ListsYour own lists (bus routes, PTA…) added in Input Data Mode's last step. Each row is matched to a student or guardian; what you keep shows under Additional Information and as Student List / Parents Divided columns.
Additional InformationThe lines from Your Lists on a phone contact, above Communications. Kept in the Backend tab and rewritten by every build.

Getting this into Google Drive

You can keep and share this guide as a Google Doc in a couple of ways:

Option A — Upload and convert (recommended)

  1. Go to drive.google.com and open the folder where you want it.
  2. Drag the PC_Tracker_User_Guide.docx file into the window (or click New → File upload).
  3. Right-click the uploaded file → Open with → Google Docs. Drive converts it to a Google Doc; the Jump-to buttons and table of contents stay clickable.
  4. (Optional) Delete the original .docx once the Google Doc is created.

Option B — From inside Google Docs

  1. Open docs.google.com and start a blank document.
  2. Choose File → Open → Upload, and drop in the .docx.
